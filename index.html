Pourable privacy policy

Pourable – Cocktails From Your Bar · Effective 26 September 2026

In short: Pourable has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your bar, favorites, recipes and photos stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads. In Iceland and the rest of the EEA you are asked for consent first.

Who we are

Pourable is made by Ellert Arason, an individual developer in Iceland, who is responsible for this policy. Contact: ellert.arason@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

The following is saved only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • the ingredients you add to “My Bar”,
  • your favorite cocktails,
  • your own recipes, including any photos you add to them,
  • your language choice.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. If you use your phone’s own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup), the backup may include it, under Apple’s or Google’s terms.

Camera and photos

The app only uses the camera or your photo library when you choose to add a photo to one of your own recipes. The photo is stored in the app on your phone. We never upload it.

Cocktail data

Cocktail recipes and pictures come from TheCocktailDB. The app downloads updates and pictures from their servers. As with any website, their servers receive your IP address when this happens. No other information about you is sent.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pourable is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area (including Iceland), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent when you first open it. Personalized ads are only shown if you agree; otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app menu → Ad privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • On iPhone, Pourable does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Read more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Age

Pourable is about alcoholic drinks and is intended for adults of legal drinking age where they live (20 in Iceland). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

We do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete: removing the app removes everything it stored. For data Google collects for advertising, use Google’s tools or contact Google. You can contact us with any question, and you have the right to complain to the Icelandic Data Protection Authority, Persónuvernd.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
